# Pulse-ED-ICA-Project
BINF301 Information and Communication Architecture Course Project - GIU
Project Title: Student Academic Health & Early-Warning Intervention System

Problem:
Professors often struggle to identify students at risk of failing until major assessments, such as midterms. Manually tracking grades and calculating students' academic standing is also time-consuming.

Proposed Solution:
A web-based system that helps instructors monitor students' academic performance and identify those at risk of failing early.

Main Features:

- Risk Classification: Automatically categorizes students into Safe, Warning, or Critical Risk based on their grades.
- Automated Alerts: Notifies professors when a student reaches the Critical Risk level.
- TA Office Hours: Assigns at-risk students mandatory TA support sessions.
- Progress Tracking: Simulates weekly academic improvement and updates students' risk levels as their grades improve.

Technologies:
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, using functions, conditions, weighted calculations, and loops.

Project Goal:
To help professors identify struggling students earlier, monitor their progress, and provide timely academic support before they fail the course.
